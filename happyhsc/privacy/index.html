Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Happy HSC ("the app") is made by Toxgam ("we"). You can contact us at toxgamgroup@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not run servers for the app, and the app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. We do not collect, receive or share your name, email address, location, contacts or usage data.

What stays on your device

Your HSC year, your chosen subjects, which practice questions you have attempted, your self-ratings, the questions you save, and the date you first opened the app are stored on your device only. If you have your phone’s backup turned on (iCloud or Google backup), it may include this data; Apple or Google control that, not us.

Keeping the first-open date

To stop the 10 free days from starting again every time the app is reinstalled, the app also keeps the date you first opened it, and nothing else, in a place that survives a reinstall: the iOS Keychain on iPhone, and Google Play’s Block Store on Android. Block Store is a Google service and is only used if you have Google backup turned on. This date does not identify you.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We do not see your payment details, name or email. The app asks the store whether your subscription is active so it can unlock the paid content; we receive no personal information from this.

Things you choose to do

  • Study reminders. If you allow them, reminders are local notifications made on your device, not sent from a server.
  • Progress report and printing. If you create a progress report or print a topic, the app makes a PDF on your device. It goes nowhere unless you choose to share it, for example by message or email, with the person you pick.

Children

The app is intended for secondary school students. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect any from children.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and describe the change on this page.
