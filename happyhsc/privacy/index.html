Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Happy HSC ("the app", and the website at happy-hsc.pages.dev) is made by Toxgam ("we"). You can contact us at toxgamgroup@gmail.com.

What we collect

We do not ask for your contacts or precise location, and there is no advertising. You do not need an account to use the iPhone and Android apps, and they do not send us your answers. The website has accounts: you log in with Google to save your answers and see the worked solutions, and then we keep the data described under "The website and accounts" below. Everything in the rest of this page about the app applies to the apps.

The app does send anonymous usage data to Amplitude, a product analytics service, so we can see how many people use the app and which features help. This is what is sent:

  • events such as opening the app, finishing setup, opening a lesson, attempting a question (the subject and how it went, never your answer), seeing or starting the subscription, using reminders, sharing a report or printing;
  • basic facts about the app and device: the app version, iPhone or Android, the HSC year and number of subjects you chose, and whether you are in the free days or subscribed;
  • a random identifier that the analytics service creates on your device. It is not your name, your Apple ID or Google account, or your phone’s advertising ID, and it is not linked to anything that identifies you;
  • your IP address, from which Amplitude works out an approximate location (country and region). We do not ask your phone for your location.

We never send your answers, drawings, notes or anything you type. We use this data only to understand and improve the app, not for advertising or tracking you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell it. Amplitude processes it for us under its own privacy policy, and it is stored in the United States. We keep it for up to 13 months. To ask us to delete the data for your device, email us and tell us roughly when you used the app.

The website and accounts

You can read the questions on the website without logging in. To answer a question or see a solution you log in with Google. From Google we receive your name, email address and Google account ID. We never receive your Google password or contacts.

For your account we store:

  • your answers: what you chose, typed or drew, whether it was right, your self-rating, and when;
  • your progress: your HSC year and subjects, which questions you have attempted and when, and questions you saved to retry;
  • your subscriptions: which subject, the status and the renewal date;
  • the share links you make and, for each, the people who opened it while logged in (their email address).

The website also keeps your login and a copy of your progress in your browser’s storage. Logging out removes your progress from that browser; it stays in your account. The website does not send the anonymous usage data described above, and it does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Where it is kept. Your account data is stored with Supabase, in a database in Sydney, Australia. The website itself is delivered by Cloudflare. We do not sell your data and we do not use your answers for advertising.

Sharing your answers

On the website you can make a share link for a page of questions, a subject or all subjects. Anyone you send the link to can, after logging in, see your latest answers on those pages and your name (as in your Google account, not your email address). They never see the correct answers or solutions and cannot change anything. A link opened without logging in shows only the questions. You can see who has opened each link, block a person, and delete a link at any time; deleting it ends access for everyone at once. Only share a link with people you trust, because anyone who has it can open it.

Payments on the website

Subscriptions bought on the website are processed by Stripe. We never see your card number. Stripe keeps payment records as the law requires. We keep which subject you subscribed to, the status and the dates. Prices include GST.

Your choices

  • Delete your account. On the website go to Settings, then Account, then "Delete my account and data". It removes your answers, progress, share links and account for good. A running subscription has to be cancelled first, so nobody is charged with no account to give it to.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or to correct it, by emailing us. We reply within a month.
  • Log out at any time in Settings.

What stays on your device

Your HSC year, your chosen subjects, which practice questions you have attempted, your self-ratings, the questions you save, and the date you first opened the app are stored on your device only. If you have your phone’s backup turned on (iCloud or Google backup), it may include this data; Apple or Google control that, not us.

Keeping the first-open date

To stop the 10 free days from starting again every time the app is reinstalled, the app also keeps the date you first opened it, and nothing else, in a place that survives a reinstall: the iOS Keychain on iPhone, and Google Play’s Block Store on Android. Block Store is a Google service and is only used if you have Google backup turned on. This date does not identify you.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We do not see your payment details, name or email. The app asks the store whether your subscription is active so it can unlock the paid content; we receive no personal information from this. The usage data above only says whether you are subscribed, not who you are.

Things you choose to do

  • Study reminders. If you allow them, reminders are local notifications made on your device, not sent from a server.
  • Progress report and printing. If you create a progress report or print a topic, the app makes a PDF on your device. It goes nowhere unless you choose to share it, for example by message or email, with the person you pick.

Children

The app and the website are intended for secondary school students, mostly aged 16 to 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from young children. The usage data above is anonymous. A student who makes a share link chooses who receives it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and describe the change on this page.
