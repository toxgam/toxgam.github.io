Happy HSC

Daily practice for the NSW HSC.

Happy HSC gives Year 11 and Year 12 students a plan for each day, unlimited original practice questions in the style of recent HSC exams, and lessons for every syllabus topic. It works offline, has no ads, and needs no account.

Get Happy HSC

Download the iPhone and iPad app on the App Store. It works offline and needs no account. You can also use Happy HSC in your web browser (log in with Google to save your answers); on an iPhone the app is the better choice.

What costs money

Every question is free. For your first 10 days everything is open. After that, the full worked solutions, the lesson text and printing or sharing PDFs of the questions need a subscription for that subject: A$5 a month for each subject, or A$15 a month for all subjects. Subscribe to any 3 subjects and every subject unlocks automatically. The store shows the exact price for your country, and you can cancel at any time. The full terms are on the Terms of use page.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas: toxgamgroup@gmail.com. To cancel or manage your subscription, use your Apple ID subscriptions (iPhone) or Google Play subscriptions (Android); see the Terms of use for the steps.
