Happy HSC

Daily practice for the NSW HSC.

Happy HSC gives Year 11 and Year 12 students a plan for each day, unlimited original practice questions in the style of recent HSC exams, and lessons for every syllabus topic. It works offline, has no ads, and needs no account.

What costs money

Every question is free. For your first 10 days everything is open. After that, the full worked solutions and the lesson text need the Happy HSC subscription, A$5 a month (the store shows the exact price for your country). New subscribers may get a free first month from the App Store or Google Play. You can cancel at any time. The full terms are on the Terms of use page.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas: toxgamgroup@gmail.com. To cancel or manage your subscription, use your Apple ID subscriptions (iPhone) or Google Play subscriptions (Android); see the Terms of use for the steps.
