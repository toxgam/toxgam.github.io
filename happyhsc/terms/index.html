What the app is

Happy HSC is a study aid for NSW HSC students. It contains original practice questions written for the app, inspired by the style of past HSC exams, along with worked solutions and lessons. It is not affiliated with NESA. We do our best to be accurate, but the content may contain mistakes, and the app does not guarantee any exam result. Always check the official NESA syllabus and your teachers for what is examined.

What is free, and what is the subscription
